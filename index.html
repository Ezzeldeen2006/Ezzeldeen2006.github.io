About

Short version

I'm a computer engineering student at AAST Cairo, graduating January 2028, and a backend intern on the Bill Payment team at Aman Holding — Java, Spring Boot, Hibernate and MySQL. I also do machine learning work, and I placed 38th nationally in the 2025 Egyptian Collegiate Programming Contest, advancing to the ECPC Finals.

The games are framed here the way I actually think about them: state machines, timing windows and AI behaviour, not genres. The API serving this page runs on an old laptop in my apartment, deliberately — constraints are more interesting than headroom. More about that.
