AtomBall Climber

Rigidbody-driven 3D physics arcade game with camera-relative control, a continuous hazard spawner, and slope-gated jump logic.

  • C#
  • Game Physics
  • Unity
Repository ↗
Play in your browser

Keyboard and mouse · 34 MB download

Systems implemented

  • Rigidbody movement driven by forces, with input relative to the camera, so W always rolls the ball away from where you're looking.
  • Cinemachine FreeLook camera that pulls in instead of clipping into the terrain.
  • Jumping that checks the slope under the ball, not just whether it's touching the ground, so you can't hop up a cliff face.
  • Asteroid spawner that keeps dropping rocks, cleans each one up after a set lifetime, and makes sure a rock can only hit you once.
  • Laser shooting aimed along the ground plane, with continuous collision detection so a shot moving at 120 units per second can't pass through an asteroid without hitting it.
  • Moving-platform finish: landing on top of the sliding platform parents the ball to it and ends the level.
  • A HUD built entirely in code: title card, timer, fall counter and finish screen with your best time. It only reads the game state, so none of the mechanics set by the assignment brief change.

Built for ECE4208 (Game Programming). All gameplay scripts and systems are mine; models, terrain heightmaps, particle prefabs, and animation clips were supplied by the course.

The story

You're a glowing ball rolling across a hilly forest. Steer around the holes, shoot down the asteroids falling out of the sky, and land on the platform sliding back and forth at the end.

How to play

Get to the end and land on top of the moving platform.

W A S D
Roll (relative to the camera)
Mouse
Move the camera around the ball
Shift
Roll faster
Space
Jump (only on fairly flat ground)
Left click
Shoot asteroids before they land
R
Play again after you finish

Falling into a hole or getting hit by an asteroid sends you back to the start, so it's worth shooting the asteroids ahead of you. Your time and number of falls are in the top-left corner.
